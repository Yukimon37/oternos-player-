Privacy Policy

This Policy explains how OTERNOS handles data for the desktop player, OTERNOS Friends service, and OTERNOS Discord bot.

OTERNOS is a small independent project. It does not sell personal data or use it for advertising. We only use data needed to operate the features you choose to use.

1. Data We Process

Depending on the features you use, OTERNOS may process:

  • Discord account identifiers, display name, and avatar after you choose Discord sign-in;
  • your OTERNOS profile name, friend code, friendship requests, and friend connections;
  • optional listening activity, such as a track title and artist, when activity sharing is enabled;
  • Friends chat messages and shared links that you send through the Service;
  • Discord bot commands, link or search text, guild and channel identifiers, and voice-channel state needed to carry out a command; and
  • limited diagnostic information used to troubleshoot reliability, abuse, or errors.

2. Local Music Stays Local

Music files in your local library remain on your device. OTERNOS does not upload your local audio files to the Friends service or Discord bot merely because they are in your library. A feature may process a link, metadata, or a file only after you specifically use that feature.

3. How Data Is Used

Data is used to provide the requested feature, including to:

  • sign you in and keep your Friends profile connected to Discord;
  • show friends, requests, chat, parties, and opted-in listening activity;
  • respond to Discord bot commands and voice playback requests;
  • protect the Service, diagnose errors, and maintain reliability; and
  • comply with applicable law or enforce the Terms of Service.

4. Sharing

OTERNOS shares data only as needed for the feature: with Discord when you use Discord sign-in or the bot, with the services you choose to search or open, and with friends when you choose to share activity, send a chat message, or join a party. We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

5. Voice and Media

The bot may send audio to a voice channel when requested. It is not designed to record voice conversations. You are responsible for having permission to play or share media in a Discord server or voice channel.

6. Your Choices

You can:

  • avoid Discord sign-in and Friends features entirely;
  • turn off listening-activity sharing in OTERNOS Settings;
  • sign out of the Friends service;
  • remove local OTERNOS data from your own device; and
  • ask the project maintainer about account-data removal through the project contact route below.

7. Data Retention and Security

Data is kept only for as long as reasonably needed to run the relevant feature, maintain accounts and conversations, address abuse, or meet legal obligations. Reasonable steps are taken to protect Service data, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

OTERNOS is not directed at children under the minimum age required to use Discord in their location. Do not use the Service if you do not meet Discord's applicable age rules.

9. Changes and Contact

This Policy may change as OTERNOS changes. The current version will remain at this URL. For questions or data requests, use the OTERNOS GitHub issue tracker and avoid posting private account information publicly.
